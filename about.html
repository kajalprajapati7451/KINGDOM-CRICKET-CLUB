Our Journey

From humble beginnings to championship glory

2010

Foundation of Kingdom CC

Kingdom Cricket Club was established in 2010 by a group of passionate cricket enthusiasts with a vision to create a professional cricket club that nurtures local talent.

2013

First Championship Win

Won our first district-level championship, marking the beginning of a successful journey in competitive cricket.

2016

Professional Academy Launch

Established the Kingdom Cricket Academy to train young talents with professional coaching facilities and structured training programs.

2019

State Level Recognition

Achieved recognition at state level competitions and produced our first state team player from the club.

2023

Premier League Champions

Crowned champions of the Premier Cricket League, our biggest achievement to date, showcasing our growth and commitment to excellence.

Our Mission

To develop world-class cricketers through professional training, state-of-the-art facilities, and a culture of excellence that promotes sportsmanship, discipline, and competitive spirit.

Our Vision

To become the premier cricket club in the region, recognized for producing international-level talent and setting standards in cricket development, training methodologies, and sports management.
