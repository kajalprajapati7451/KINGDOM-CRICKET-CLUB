Our Journey

From humble beginnings to championship glory

2010

Foundation of Kingdom CC

Kingdom Cricket Club was established by passionate cricket enthusiasts with a vision to create a professional cricket club.

2015

First Championship Win

Won our first district-level championship, marking the beginning of our successful journey.

2020

State Level Recognition

Achieved recognition at state level competitions and produced our first state team player.

2023

Premier League Champions

Crowned champions of the Premier Cricket League, our biggest achievement to date.

Our Mission

To develop world-class cricketers through professional training, state-of-the-art facilities, and a culture of excellence.

Our Vision

To become the premier cricket club in the region, recognized for producing international-level talent.
